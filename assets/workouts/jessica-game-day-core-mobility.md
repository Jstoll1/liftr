# Jess + Partner: Game-Day Core & Mobility

**Duration:** 30–35 minutes  
**Equipment:** Dumbbells and bodyweight  
**Effort:** Moderate/easy  
**Goal:** Core stability, mobility, and activation without leg fatigue. Designed for a basketball day.

Move with your partner. Perform paired core exercises at the same time, then switch. Finish feeling better rather than beaten up.

## 1. Warm-up together — 5 minutes

- Treadmill Walk — 3 minutes
- World’s Greatest Stretch — 3/side
- Squat-to-Stand + Overhead Reach — 6 reps
- Cat-Cow — 6 reps

## 2. Core circuit — 3 rounds

Partners perform one movement each at the same time, then switch sides. Rest 45 seconds after each round.

- DB Dead Bug Hold + Heel Taps — 8/side / Forearm Plank — 30–40 seconds
- DB Suitcase March — 10/side / Bird Dog — 8/side
- Side Plank — 25 seconds/side / DB Glute Bridge — 12 reps

## 3. Mobility flow — 2 rounds

Move through together side by side.

- 90/90 Hip Switches — 8/side
- Adductor Rock-Backs — 8/side
- Half-Kneeling Hip-Flexor Stretch + Reach — 30 seconds/side
- Knee-Over-Toe Ankle Rocks — 10/side
- Open-Book Rotations — 6/side

## 4. Core + activation finisher — 2 rounds

Move together with minimal rest.

- DB Goblet Squat Hold — 20 seconds
- Slow Calf Raises — 12 reps
- Plank Shoulder Taps — 8/side
- Glute Bridge March — 8/side
- Dead Hang — 20–30 seconds

## 5. Three-minute reset

- Hip Flexor Stretch — 30 seconds/side
- Adductor Stretch — 30 seconds/side
- Calf Stretch — 30 seconds/side
- Deep Breathing — 4–5 slow breaths

This is a “feel good” session rather than a workout to failure.
